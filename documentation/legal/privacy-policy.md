Prediqo is an informational football analytics application. It is not a bookmaker and
does not accept wagers or operate a betting wallet.

---

## 1. Information we collect

- **Account data:** email address, display name and Firebase user identifier when you
  register or sign in.
- **Preferences:** favourite teams/competitions, timezone, theme and notification settings.
- **Usage and diagnostics:** request logs containing IP address, user agent, route and
  timestamps, kept for operations and security.
- **Purchase data:** Google Play order identifiers and purchase tokens used to verify
  entitlements. We do not store your card details.
- **Device tokens:** push notification tokens, only if you enable notifications.
- **Sample/dev data:** development fixtures are clearly labelled `DEMO` and are never
  presented as real match data.

## 2. How we use information

To provide fixtures, predictions and analysis; maintain accounts and entitlements;
send notifications you opted into; detect abuse; and improve reliability.

We do **not** sell personal data. We do not use data for gambling or betting purposes.

## 3. Legal bases

Performance of a contract (account and purchases), legitimate interests (security and
service reliability), and consent (notifications where required).

## 4. Retention

- Account data: until you delete your account, then pseudonymised.
- Prediction records: retained as non-personal historical analytics even after account
  deletion; they are never linked back to a deleted account.
- Logs: proposed 90 days.
- Audit logs: proposed 12 months.
- Backups: proposed 30-day rolling retention.

## 5. Your rights

You may request access, correction, export or deletion of your personal data. In-app
account deletion is available under Profile → Settings. Some prediction/audit records
must be retained for integrity and legal reasons but are pseudonymised.

## 6. Third parties

- Firebase (authentication and push messaging)
- Google Play (purchase verification and subscriptions)
- Licensed football data provider (match data only; no personal data)

## 7. Children

Prediqo is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.
