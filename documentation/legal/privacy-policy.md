# Privacy Policy — Prediqo

**Last updated:** November 2026

## 1. Introduction

Prediqo ("we", "our", "us") is an AI-powered football analytics and prediction
platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your
personal information when you use our mobile application ("App") and related services.

By using Prediqo, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance
with this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
- **Email address** or **phone number** (used for sign-up and sign-in)
- **Display name** (optional, chosen by you)
- **Password** (stored as a salted hash; never stored in plain text)

### 2.2 Usage Data
- **Prediction requests** (which fixtures you viewed, when, and how many)
- **Token transactions** (purchases, unlocks, balance changes)
- **Subscription status** (VIP membership, plan, expiry)
- **Device information** (platform, OS version, device model — for compatibility)
- **Push notification tokens** (FCM tokens for delivering notifications)

### 2.3 Analytics Data
- **App usage patterns** (screens visited, features used, session duration)
- **Error logs and crash reports** (for debugging and improving stability)
- **Performance metrics** (API response times, load times)

### 2.4 Advertising Data
- **Ad interactions** (impressions, clicks — via Google AdMob)
- **Ad consent status** (whether you've consented to personalized ads)

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- **Provide and maintain** the App and its features
- **Process payments** and manage your token balance and VIP subscription
- **Send notifications** about fixtures, predictions, and account updates
- **Improve the App** through analytics and error tracking
- **Display advertisements** (where applicable, based on your consent)
- **Prevent fraud** and ensure platform security
- **Comply with legal obligations**

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- Your data is stored on secure servers with encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest
- Passwords are hashed using PBKDF2 with 120,000 iterations
- We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data
- Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only

## 5. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell your personal data. We may share data with:
- **Service providers** (Firebase for authentication, Google AdMob for ads, payment processors)
- **Legal authorities** when required by law
- **Analytics providers** (anonymized usage data only)

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access** your personal data
- **Correct** inaccurate data (via Settings → Profile)
- **Delete** your account (via Settings → Delete Account)
- **Opt out** of notifications (via Settings → Notifications)
- **Withdraw ad consent** at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@prediqo.app.

## 7. Data Retention

- Account data is retained while your account is active
- Upon account deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days
- Historical predictions are retained anonymously for integrity purposes
- Audit logs are retained for 12 months

## 8. Children's Privacy

Prediqo is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly
collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided
us with personal information, please contact us.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted in the App
and on our website. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.

## 10. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy:
- Email: privacy@prediqo.app
- Website: https://prediqo.app
